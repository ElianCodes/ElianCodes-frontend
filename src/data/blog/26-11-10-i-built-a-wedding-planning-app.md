# I built a wedding planning app for our own wedding

Moya and I are getting married on 21 May 2027.

So I did what I always do when a problem shows up. I went looking for software.

Nothing exotic. A guest list. A way for people to say if they're coming. A page that isn't a PDF. A budget that lives in one place.

What I expected:

* a planner that knows a Belgian wedding
* a guest list shaped like the invitations
* dates that line up with the town hall

What I found:

* tools built for American weddings
* checklists that have never heard of a huwelijksaangifte
* a subscription for a day that happens once

The Belgian part is the one that kept breaking the template.

You don't just pick a Saturday and send the invites. There's a marriage declaration, the huwelijksaangifte. A window. A town hall. Deadlines. Get those wrong and the date you already told people is not the date.

The planners I tried had no field for it. No reminder. Nothing.

Which makes sense, if you squint. Most of them are American. An American wedding doesn't file a declaration like that before the party. They weren't wrong about their own country. They just don't know mine.

I got tired of planning around that.

So I built [Ringske](https://ringske.be).

## What it does

* A guest list with one row per invitation, not one row per person. Everyone you invite together shares that row, and the headcount sits on it.
* One shared QR code for the RSVP. Same code, whoever you hand it to.
* Tasks that shift with the date. Move the wedding, the tasks move with it.
* A budget.
* A wedding website on its own address. Dutch, English, and French.
* Hosted in the EU.

You pay once for that wedding. Not a subscription you cancel afterwards.
